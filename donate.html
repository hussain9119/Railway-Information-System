Before you donate

Eligibility depends on your health, age, weight, medicines and local guidelines. A qualified medical team will assess you before donation.

  • Contact an authorised blood centre.
  • Answer health-screening questions honestly.
  • Follow the staff’s instructions before and after donation.
  • Carry valid identification if requested by the centre.
This website cannot register real donors. Use an authorised blood donation centre.
