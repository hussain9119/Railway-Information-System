Every drop can make a difference.

A student-built awareness website concept for blood donation in Kurnool.

Our purpose

This project demonstrates how a simple website could present blood donation information, sample blood-group availability and request forms in one place.

Hussain Blood Bank is a front-end demonstration only. It is not a licensed blood bank, hospital, or emergency response service.

Voluntary Donation

Encouraging people to learn about safe, voluntary blood donation.

Safety First

Donation screening and collection must be handled by qualified medical professionals.

Community

Awareness and verified local services can help communities stay informed.
